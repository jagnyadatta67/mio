🌾 Finger Millet (Ragi/Mandia) Idli/Dosa/Uttapam/Paniyaram

Ingredients:

  • Finger millet (Ragi/Madua/Mandia/Nachni) – 3 cups
  • Black gram (urad) dal – 1 cup
  • Fenugreek seeds (methi) – 1 tsp
  • Flattened Rice (Poha) – ¼ cup
  • Water
  • Salt as per taste

For tempering (Optional):

  • Cooking oil – 1 tbsp
  • Mustard seeds – 1 tsp
  • Gram (chana) Dal – 1 tsp
  • Black gram (urad) Dal – 1 tsp
  • Curry leaves – 10-15
  • Chopped green chillies – 2
  • Grated ginger – 1 inch

For vegetable idli/uttapam/paniyaram (Optional):

  • Chopped Vegetables – carrot, onion, beans, capsicum, coriander leaves.

Method:

  1. Step 1: Wash thoroughly and soak the millet, urad dal, Poha, and Fenugreek seeds in water for 6-7 hours (use the same cup for measuring all ingredients).
  2. Step 2: Grind into fine paste, mix using hands and keep overnight for fermentation. This fermented batter can be stored in fridge and used in small batches for idli/dosa/uttapam/paniyaram.
  3. Step 3: Add salt and make your idli, dosa, uttapam or paniyaram as per choice. You may like to use mustard seeds, urad dal, chana dal, curry leaves, green chillies, grated ginger for tempering and add vegetables (optional) in batter for vegetable idli/uttapam/paniyaram.

Variant:

You may use Foxtail Millet, Little Millet, Pear Millet, Kodo Millet etc. in place of Finger Millet and follow the same process for making idli/dosa/uttapam/paniyaram.
